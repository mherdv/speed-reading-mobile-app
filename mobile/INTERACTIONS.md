# Interaction Map (Typing + Selecting)

This document lists every place in the app where the user must *type* or *select/tap* something to proceed.

## Screens

### ExerciseScreen

- Tap: `cancel` (Back)
- Tap: `start-reading` (Start timer)
- Tap: `finish-reading` (Move to question phase)
- Tap: `choice-0..N` (Select one answer)
- Tap: `back-to-reading` (Return to reading)
- Tap: `submit-answer` (Finish attempt and compute WPM)

### ResultScreen

- Tap: `done` (Back to home)
- Tap: `history` (Open history)

### HistoryScreen

- Tap: Back button (calls `onBack`)
- Tap: Past results (if present in UI; depends on current implementation)
